01 / OUR PURPOSE

A basic need.
Not a barrier.

Nobody should have to choose between managing their period and showing up for their life.

When period products are out of reach, an ordinary day can become a difficult choice: ask for help, make do, or go home.

Donation drives provide valuable support. Hope Hub builds on that work with something people can return to — a place to get what they need, when they need it.

Because the need doesn’t end when a campaign does.

GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity
goes a long way.

Something else on your mind?
Ask our team ↗

Are the period products really free?+

Yes. Products supplied through Hope Hub units are free at the point of use. No payment or proof of need is required.

Do I need an app or an account?+

No. The Hub is designed for direct access using the control on the unit. No app, account or registration needed.

Can I suggest a location for a Hub?+

Absolutely. Tell us about the location, the community it serves and who manages the space. Our team can then discuss suitability and next steps.

Can my organisation get involved?+

We welcome conversations about funding units, supporting supplies, hosting a Hub and contributing specialist skills. Use the enquiry form below to introduce your organisation.

What makes a good host location?+

A suitable shared space, a local contact and a practical plan for access and ongoing supplies. Specific installation and restocking responsibilities need to be agreed for each location.
